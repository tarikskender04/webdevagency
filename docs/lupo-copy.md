## 3. How it works

**Eyebrow:** How it works

## Stop explaining screenshots.

Telling an agent about a visual problem usually means screenshot, crop, paste, explain, and
again for the next one. With Lupo you just talk and point, the way you would with someone
sitting next to you.

1. **Press ⌥⇧Space and talk.**
   Say what you'd tell a colleague looking over your shoulder. No need to write a perfect prompt.

2. **Press ⌥⇧S to mark it.**
   The screen freezes. Circle it or draw an arrow, then keep talking. Switch apps as often as you like.

3. **Press Done.**
   Lupo writes a `context.md` with your words and the marked-up screenshots in order, and hands
   it to Claude Code in VS Code, Cursor or a terminal, or to Codex. Or copy it and paste it anywhere.

## 4. Privacy

**Eyebrow:** Private by design

## It all stays on your Mac.

- **On-device transcription.** macOS turns your voice into text on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
- **No account, no cloud.** Nothing to sign up for. Your sessions are saved in a folder on your Mac.
- **No AI rewriting.** Your agent gets exactly what you said and showed. Lupo adds nothing.

Lupo only goes online to check for updates. What you hand to your agent is up to you.

## 5. First launch

**Eyebrow:** Installing

## Opening Lupo the first time

Lupo isn't from the App Store and hasn't been notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to
confirm it the first time you open it. It takes about a minute, and you only do it once.

1. **Drag Lupo into Applications.**
   Open `Lupo.dmg` and drag Lupo onto the Applications folder.

2. **Open Lupo.**
   macOS says it can't verify the app. Click **Done**. That's expected.

3. **Click Open Anyway.**
   Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll down to Security. Next to the message about Lupo, click **Open Anyway**.

4. **Confirm it.**
   Use Touch ID or your password, then click **Open Anyway** once more. Lupo's wolf appears in your menu bar.

## 6. Permissions

**Eyebrow:** Permissions

## What Lupo asks for, and why

- **Microphone.** To hear your narration. It's transcribed on your Mac.
- **Screen Recording.** To take the screenshots you capture. Lupo only grabs the screen when you
  press capture. macOS asks you to reopen Lupo after you allow this one.
- **Accessibility (optional).** Only for auto-paste, so Lupo can press ⌘V in your agent's chat
  for you. You still press Return yourself. Skip it and Lupo copies to the clipboard instead.

**Eyebrow:** Why I built it

## I built Lupo for myself first.

I'm Tarik Skender. I run Prodigy Stdio, a web studio in Sarajevo, and most of my days are spent
building sites with a coding agent.

Most of what I ask for is visual: this spacing, that headline, the button over there. I'd end the
day with a desktop full of screenshots and a chat full of paragraphs explaining them. The
screenshot was never the hard part. Explaining it was.

Showing the same thing to someone sitting next to me took seconds. So I built Lupo to work that
way: I talk, I point, and the agent gets the full picture.
